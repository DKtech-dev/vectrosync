## 3. Layer 3: Physics-Trained Neural Operating-Point Surrogate

### Architectural Scope & Engineering Role
- **Model Type:** Two-Layer Fully Connected MLP (4 inputs $\\to$ 32 $\\to$ 32 $\\to$ 5 outputs).
- **Prediction Scope:** Predicts 5 scalar dynamic extrema and kinematic outputs:
  1. Peak Polished Rod Load ($PPRL$, kN)
  2. Minimum Polished Rod Load ($MPRL$, kN)
  3. Minimum Downhole Plunger Tension ($F_{min}$, kN) — the primary rod float/buckling constraint
  4. Mean Downhole Plunger Tension ($F_{mean}$, kN)
  5. Net Effective Plunger Stroke ($S_{plunger}$, m)
- **Important Distinction:** This surrogate does **not** predict the continuous spatio-temporal displacement field $u(x,t)$ across the rod string; that remains governed by the 116-node Gibbs-damped hyperbolic wave PDE solver. The surrogate operates as an inner-loop screening engine for MPC speed candidates.
- **Full PDE Wave Solver Runtime:** ~304.3 ms per stroke (116 spatial nodes, 8,194 subcycles).
- **Neural Operating-Point Surrogate Runtime:** **0.006 ms** (5.9 $\mu$s).
- **Acceleration Factor:** **~51,692× faster** than full finite-difference time integration.
- **MPC Capability:** Evaluates 50 candidate pump speeds in < 0.3 ms, well within the 100 ms edge PLC control budget.
